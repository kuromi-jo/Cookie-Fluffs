Our story

From one tiny oven to your doorstep

Cookie Fluffs is a small Quezon City bakery on a sweet mission: the fluffiest cookie you've ever had.

Cookie Fluffs began with a simple question: why can't a cookie be crispy, chewy and fluffy all at once? Many test batches later, we found our answer. It's a thick, soft-centered cookie, generously loaded and topped with our signature white heart.
