FAQ

Quick answers

Helpful details about your account, orders, payments and more.

Do I need an account to browse?

No. You can explore the whole menu as a guest. Log in when you want to save favorites, keep a cart, manage addresses, use vouchers or place an order.

Where are my favorite cookies saved?

Favorites belong to your Cookie Fluffs account, so they come back after you log in again. A cookie that is off the menu can stay in your favorites, but it can’t be added to your cart until it returns.

How are prices calculated?

Prices, promo prices, box discounts, voucher savings and the delivery fee all come from Cookie Fluffs when you check out, so you always pay today’s price. Your order keeps a copy of the items and totals you accepted.

Where do you deliver?

Delivery is available in approved areas of Metro Manila (NCR). You can also choose store pickup at checkout.

How do I pay?

Pay in cash on delivery or pickup, or online with GCash or Maya (PayMongo test mode in this class project). Cash orders show “Pay on handover” and change to Paid once the order is completed. Online payments are marked Paid only after PayMongo confirms them; coming back from the payment page alone does not count.

How do I use a voucher?

Open My Vouchers, copy a code, then apply it in the Review step at checkout. Some vouchers only work on certain cookies, like the Halloween specials.

How can I track my order?

Open the order in My Orders. You’ll see every status update as it happens, and for deliveries a map shows the rider leaving our kitchen and heading to your address. The bell on Home tells you whenever your order changes.

Can I cancel an order?

Before we start preparing, tap Cancel order on Order details and it is cancelled right away. Once preparing has started, you can send a cancellation request that Cookie Fluffs approves or declines; a declined request is final. After your order is ready, it can no longer be cancelled.

Where can I check a refund?

If a paid order is cancelled, Order details shows its refund review status. A cancellation does not automatically mean a refund has been sent.

How do I get a receipt?

Open the order and tap View & print receipt. From there, Print or save as PDF opens your phone’s print screen, where you can save the receipt as a PDF.

How do reviews work?

Once an order is completed, you can rate each cookie in it and add a comment. Reviews appear on the cookie’s page for everyone to see, and you can choose to post anonymously.

When are the Halloween specials available?

Halloween cookies can be ordered from October 1 to November 30. Outside those dates they are taken off the menu.

Can I change my saved address after ordering?

Yes. Editing a saved address does not change the delivery details already stored with an order you placed.

I forgot my password. What do I do?

Tap Forgot password on the login screen and enter your email. We’ll send you a 6-digit code that works for 30 minutes; enter it with your new password. To change your password while logged in, go to Privacy & security; we’ll also email you a code to confirm it’s you.

How do I change app readability?

Open App Settings from your account to reduce motion or use larger text. If your phone is set to reduce motion, the app follows that too.
