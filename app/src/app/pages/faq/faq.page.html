FAQ

Quick answers

Helpful details about your account, orders, and payments.

Do I need an account to browse?

No. You can explore cookies as a guest. Log in when you want to save favorites, keep a cart, manage addresses, or place an order.

Where are my favorite cookies saved?

Favorites belong to your Cookie Fluffs account, so they return after you log in again. An unavailable cookie can stay saved, but it cannot be added to the cart.

How are prices calculated?

The app asks Cookie Fluffs for current product prices, pack discounts, and delivery fees. The final order keeps a snapshot of the accepted items and totals.

How do online payment updates work?

The order page shows the payment state verified by Cookie Fluffs. Returning from a payment page alone does not mark an order paid.

Can I cancel an order?

Cancellation options depend on the current fulfillment stage. The Order Details page shows the action currently allowed. A paid cancellation does not automatically mean a refund was issued.

Where can I review a refund?

Eligible cancelled orders show their refund-review status in Order Details. Cookie Fluffs records review updates separately from the original payment.

Can I change my saved address after ordering?

Yes. Editing a saved address does not rewrite the delivery details already stored with an existing order.

How do I change app readability?

Open App Settings from your profile to reduce nonessential motion or use larger text. Your device's reduced-motion request is always respected.
